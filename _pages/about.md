## My story

I am a Business Analytics and Information Management graduate student at Purdue University's Daniels School of Business. I began in computer science and then developed a deeper interest in using data to understand operational problems, evaluate options, and support decisions.

My experience has included analytics work in maritime technology, business analysis, and a Purdue industry practicum. Across these settings, I have worked with technical and business stakeholders, structured complex data, built analytical workflows, and communicated findings in a way that supports practical next steps.

I am especially interested in business analytics, product and operations analytics, and roles where analytical thinking connects directly to business decisions.
