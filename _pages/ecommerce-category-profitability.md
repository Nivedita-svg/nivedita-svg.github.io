**Tools:** SQL, Google BigQuery  
**Period:** Fall 2026

## The question

Does the category that sells the most also generate the most profit? This analysis compared product-category volume with revenue, profit, and margin to understand where commercial performance was strongest.

## Data

The project used an e-commerce dataset queried in Google BigQuery. The analysis compared 26 categories and examined 2024–2025 profit growth.

## Approach

- Wrote SQL queries to aggregate units, revenue, profit, and margin by category.
- Compared category popularity with profitability.
- Examined profit growth across the 2024–2025 period.

## Findings

- The highest-volume category had a **46.8% profit margin**.
- **Outerwear & Coats** generated the highest profit at **$121K**, with a **55.6% margin**.

These figures are reproduced from the project summary available for this portfolio. No additional performance figures have been inferred.

## What this illustrates

Sales volume alone does not tell the full story of category performance. Looking at profit and margin alongside units and revenue gives decision-makers a more useful basis for prioritization.
