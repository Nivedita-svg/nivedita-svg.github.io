## E-commerce Category Profitability — “Popularity Isn't Always Profit”

**SQL · Google BigQuery · Business Analytics**

An analysis of category-level sales and profitability, comparing units sold, revenue, profit, margin, and year-over-year profit growth across 26 e-commerce categories.

- Compared volume and profitability rather than treating sales volume as a proxy for business value.
- Identified a 46.8% margin for the highest-volume category.
- Found that Outerwear & Coats generated the highest profit: **$121K at a 55.6% margin**.

## AI-Driven Student Recruitment Management Platform
