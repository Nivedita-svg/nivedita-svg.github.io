**Tools:** NLP, Python, Flask, Google Gemini Pro  
**Context:** Academic research project

## The problem

Students and placement teams need a more structured way to compare candidate resumes with job descriptions and support interview preparation.

## Approach

- Developed NLP-based machine-learning algorithms for resume-to-job-description matching.
- Delivered the matching functionality through a Flask web application.
- Integrated Google Gemini Pro with prompt engineering to support an automated interview module.

## What I learned

The project brought together natural-language processing, application development, and generative AI in a recruitment workflow. It also reinforced the importance of connecting model outputs to a user-facing process.
