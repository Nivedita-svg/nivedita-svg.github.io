From computer science to business decisions

My story

I started in computer science in Mumbai, and kept being drawn to the same question: once you have the data, what should the business actually do?

That question led me to a postgraduate diploma in Data Science and Business Analytics, and then to two years as a data analyst in maritime technology. At VSL Marine Technology I worked with marine engineers to turn engine and inspection data into dashboards for maintenance planning. At JiBe Data Analytics I led work between data-entry teams and engineers to keep vessel records accurate and to build reporting and data-import workflows for ship-management clients.

I’m now a Master’s student in Business Analytics and Information Management at Purdue’s Daniels School of Business. On my industry practicum I work as an analytics consultant, confirming operating rules with the client, owning our team’s baseline comparison and building cancellation-risk models.

I’m looking for roles in business analytics and in product and operations analytics, where analytical work connects directly to a business decision.
