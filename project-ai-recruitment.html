Case study · NLP & generative AI

AI-driven student recruitment platform

An NLP engine that matches student résumés to job descriptions, plus an automated interview module powered by Google Gemini Pro, published as a co-authored research paper.

Role
Co-author & developer
When
April 2024
Tools
Python · NLP · Flask · Gemini Pro
Output
Published paper

The problem

Students and placement teams need a structured way to compare résumés with job descriptions and to help students prepare for interviews.

Approach

  • Developed an NLP-based résumé-to-job matching engine to help students find suitable placements.
  • Delivered the matching as a Flask web application.
  • Integrated Google Gemini Pro through prompt engineering into an automated AI interview module for student candidates.

Research

Co-authored a published research paper (ISBN 978-81-978782-2-0) and presented “AI-Driven Placement Management System” at the International Conference on AI Horizons, HSNC University (2023–24).

What I learned

The project brought natural-language processing, application development and generative AI together in one recruitment workflow. It also showed me that model outputs matter most when they connect to a process a real user follows.
